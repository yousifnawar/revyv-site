Revyv

Revyv is an iPhone app for people who track their health with a wearable. It reads a WHOOP strap, an Apple Watch or a Fitbit and shows sleep, recovery and strain scores, workouts, nutrition and trends, with an optional Friends tab to share your day with friends.

Your data stays on your iPhone. Fitbit wearers can connect their Google account so Revyv reads the band's heart rate, sleep and HRV directly, read-only.
